Ali Tailor – About Us

Digital Tailor Management Book

Ali Tailor app is essentially a modern and complete system for tailors, which digitizes the traditional copy-pen methodology. Our goal is to provide all our tailor brothers with this complete facility so that they can use this tailor management app, which makes their work much easier, whether it is about accounting or managing customer registration.

Key Features

  • Account & Credit Tracking: Easily keep track of credit accounts and advance payments during booking.
  • Media Attachments: Add design and fabric images directly during bookings.
  • Customizable Registration: Edit the customer registration form as per your convenience.
  • Flexible Booking Management: Make changes to measurements and modify order receipts later with complete ease.
  • Communication Tools: Built-in WhatsApp and phone call facilities along with PDF sharing.
  • Backup & Notifications: Complete data backup and important alerts for order tracking, upcoming deliveries, and credit notifications.
  • Multilingual Support: Available in seven languages — Urdu, English, Hindi, Bengali, Nepali, Arabic, and Farsi.

Simple & User-Friendly

The interface is designed to be very simple and extremely easy to understand, making the digital transition effortless for every user.
